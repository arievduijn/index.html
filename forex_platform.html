Founder & Analyst
Arie E.F. van Duijn

Arie E.F. van Duijn is the founder of Van Duijn Markets, an independent platform providing professional technical analysis and market bias on the forex market.

With over four years of experience in the financial markets, Van Duijn Markets operates with a structured and disciplined approach. Alongside managing personal capital, the firm is active with multiple proprietary trading firms, including FTMO, where discipline, risk management, and consistency are core principles.

The analysis provided by Van Duijn Markets is grounded in the concept that financial markets continuously seek balance, and that price inefficiencies are eventually filled over time. The firm's approach focuses on liquidity, market structure, and identifying high-probability zones within the forex market.

Van Duijn Markets aims to promote a more professional and business-focused view of trading — moving away from the noise and misleading culture that has become prevalent in the industry.
